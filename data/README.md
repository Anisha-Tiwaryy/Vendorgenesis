Put the data.gov.in "Registrars of Companies (RoC)-wise Company Master Data" CSV for Telangana
(RoC Hyderabad) in data/raw/ and run:

    python evaluate.py --registry data/raw/<file>.csv

Raw files are git-ignored. Source: https://www.data.gov.in/resource/registrars-companies-roc-wise-company-master-data
